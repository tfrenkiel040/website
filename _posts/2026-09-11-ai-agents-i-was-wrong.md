In June last year (2025), I wrote about [AI agents and accountability](/ai/2025/06/14/ai-agents-accountability.html). Here are a few of my claims from back then:

- AI agents seem to be the next step in AI development
- A lot of new AI agent startups are being founded
- I still haven't seen compelling examples of AI agents that are genuinely useful in practice.
- It will take a while before we see agents really taking over work, because if you deploy an agent, you are still the one responsible for the output of the work

So how do I look back on this now?

I definitely changed my mind on some things. I'll go over them one by one.

## AI agents seem to be the next step in AI development

This was spot on. (Some people may say that when I wrote this, it was already evident.)

## A lot of new AI agent startups are being founded

This was something I was seeing, but it was anecdotal rather than empirical. I just came across a bunch of LinkedIn posts from founders in the space.

At work my team and I will, on a daily basis, say things like: "I am talking to my agent to learn something/do something" or "My agent is doing that" or "Yeah my agent did that".
