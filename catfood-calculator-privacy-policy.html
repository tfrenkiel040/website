Privacy Policy for Cat Food Calculator

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Cat Food Calculator is designed to work offline. The app does not require an account, does not contain advertising or analytics, and does not send cat profiles or food information to the developer.

Information entered into the app

You may enter information such as a cat's name, weight, neutered status, activity level, age category, body condition, target weight, and details from cat-food packaging. You may also save app preferences.

This information is used only on your device to calculate and display estimated calorie needs and food portions.

How information is stored

Information entered into the app is stored locally on your device using an on-device database and local preferences. Cat Food Calculator does not operate a user-account system or cloud database, and the developer cannot view the information you enter.

Data collection and transmission

The app does not collect or transmit personal data, cat-profile data, food data, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. It does not sell personal information or share app-entered information with advertisers or data brokers.

Device backups and app stores

Your device or operating-system backup service may include app data in a device backup, depending on your device and account settings. Such backups are controlled by the operating-system or backup provider and are subject to that provider's privacy policy. The developer does not receive or have access to those backups.

Google Play, and any future app-store provider, may process download, purchase, diagnostic, or account information independently under its own terms and privacy policy. Cat Food Calculator does not receive that information unless the store provides it through its standard developer services.

Data retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because the developer does not receive this information, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Security

The app limits privacy risk by keeping entered information on the device. Access to that information may still depend on the security of your device, operating system, backups, and any person who can unlock or access the device.

Children's privacy

Cat Food Calculator is a general-audience utility for cat owners and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, use the developer contact details or contact form provided on the website where this policy is published.
